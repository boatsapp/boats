# Waiting on the native (App Store / Play Store) app

Things BOATS can't do as a web app, to build once it ships as a native app.

## Step goal with live steps
- Optional **step goal** in setup (Goal step) and in Settings.
- Read today's steps from **Apple Health** (HealthKit) on iPhone and **Health Connect** on Android, with the user's permission.
- Show a slider/progress bar on the workout (Lift) tab: steps so far → goal, with a small celebration when the goal is hit.
- Why not now: web apps (including Home Screen apps) have no access to Apple Health or Health Connect.

## Other native-only items already discussed
- Photo picker that opens the library directly (no "Choose File" option).
- Push notifications that don't need the Home Screen install.
