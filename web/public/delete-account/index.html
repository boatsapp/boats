Delete your BOATS account

Last updated: November 27, 2026

You can permanently delete your BOATS account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BOATS and go to Profile.
  2. Tap the menu (☰) to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Walk the plank.
  4. Tap Delete account, then Delete. You can download your data first.

Without the app

Email boatsapplication@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BOATS account" from the email address you sign in with, and include your BOATS username. We will confirm and delete your account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in, username, profile, photo, bio and settings.
  • All workouts, plans, weigh-ins, progress and streak history.
  • Your posts, photos, comments, likes, pins and the messages you sent.

Data is removed from our live systems right away and from backups within 30 days. We may keep limited records when required by law or for safety, such as a record that an account was removed for breaking our rules. Messages you sent may remain visible to the person you sent them to if they saved a copy on their device.
