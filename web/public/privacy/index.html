BOATS

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

BOATS ("we", "us") is a workout tracker you use with your friends. This policy explains what information we collect when you use BOATS at carrytheboats.app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Sign-in information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile information you add. Username, name, bio, link, town, profile photo, and optional details like height, body weight, sex, goal and experience level.
  • Workout information. Your weekly split, exercises, sets, reps, weights, workout times, cardio, streaks, weigh-ins and photos you attach to workouts.
  • Social activity. Posts, captions, likes, comments, who you follow, direct messages (text, photos and voice notes), reports you send and feedback you submit.
  • Basic usage information. When you signed up and last used the app, and simple settings stored on your device (like sound and theme).

What is public and what is private

  • Private by default: your workout history, body weight, height and sex stay private unless you choose to share them. Sex is only used to estimate calories and is never shown on your profile.
  • Visible to other signed-in BOATS users: your username, name, profile photo, bio, link, posts you share publicly, likes, comments, who you follow, and anything you switch to public in Settings (such as town, goal, gym status and streak).
  • Direct messages are visible to you and the person you message.

How we use your information

  • To run BOATS: save your workouts, show your progress, build your feed, and let you follow, message and interact with friends.
  • To estimate calories and suggest weights and rep ranges.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, investigate abuse, and enforce our Terms. The BOATS team can review accounts, posts, workouts and messages when needed for safety and moderation.
  • To improve the app using the feedback you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where your information is stored

BOATS uses Google Firebase (Google Cloud) for sign-in, hosting and data storage. Your information is processed and stored on Google's servers under Google's security and privacy terms.

Sharing

We only share your information with service providers that run BOATS for us (Google Firebase), when you choose to make something public or send it to someone, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and what others can see at any time in the app.
  • Make workouts private, delete posts, and block, mute or report other users.
  • Delete your account: send a request with the Feedback button in the app. We will delete your profile, workouts, posts, likes, comments and messages.

Children

BOATS is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as BOATS grows. If we make important changes, we will let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the Feedback button in the app.
