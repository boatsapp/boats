Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BOATS ("BOATS", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares, stores and protects your information when you use the BOATS app on the web at carrytheboats.app and in the Apple App Store and Google Play versions (together, the "Service"). By using BOATS you agree to this policy.

The short version. We collect what we need to run a workout tracker with friends. We never sell your personal information. Health and fitness data is never used for advertising. Marketing emails are opt-in only. You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

BOATS is a workout tracker and fitness social app operated by its founder in the United States. For any privacy question or request, email boatsapplication@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and name from your sign-in provider (Google today, Apple in the App Store version), your username, an optional display name, profile photo, bio, link and town.
  • Body and training details: height, body weight and weigh-ins, goal weight, sex (optional, used only to estimate calories), goal, experience level, units, weekly split, plans, workouts, sets, reps, weights, rest times, cardio minutes and settings, injury flags and checklist items.
  • Content: photos and captions you post, workout pictures, comments, likes, pinned items, direct messages (text, photos and voice notes) and feedback you send us.
  • Social graph: who you follow, who follows you, people you block or hide, and reports you submit.
  • Preferences: sharing toggles, notification and email choices, reminders, theme and sound settings.

Information collected automatically

  • Activity in the app: when you were last active, whether a workout is in progress (to show your "at the gym" status to followers if you allow it), streaks and rankings.
  • Device and technical data: basic information needed to run the Service, such as app version, device type, and error details. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

We do not collect precise GPS location. Your town is something you type in, and you choose whether it is shown.

3. Health and fitness data

Workouts, body weight, height, calories and similar details are health and fitness data. We treat them with extra care:

  • We use them only to provide fitness features to you: logging, progress charts, weight suggestions, calorie estimates, streaks and the parts of your profile you choose to share.
  • We never use health and fitness data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never sell it or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect: if and when you connect Apple Health (HealthKit) or Android Health Connect, BOATS will read or write only the data types you approve (for example workouts, body weight, steps and active energy), only to provide fitness features inside BOATS. Data obtained from HealthKit or Health Connect is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold, is not shared with third parties except as needed to provide the feature you asked for, and is not stored in iCloud by BOATS. You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.
  • BOATS is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice. See our Terms.

4. Google user data

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address, profile picture URL and a unique account ID with BOATS, with your permission. We request only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, profile). We do not access your Gmail, Contacts, Drive, Calendar, Google Fit or any other Google service, and we never receive your Google password.

We use Google user data to create, sign in to and secure your BOATS account and to contact you about your account. We send marketing emails to that address only if you turn on "Emails from BOATS" in Settings (see Emails and marketing). We do not sell Google user data, use it to build advertising profiles, share it with advertisers, or use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We transfer it only to our service provider Google Firebase, which stores it for us, or when required by law.

BOATS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. How we use information

  • Run the Service: create your account, save and sync your data across devices, show your progress, streaks, rankings and profile.
  • Personalize training: suggest weights, reps, rest times and splits, and estimate calories.
  • Social features: build your feed, show likes, comments, messages, follower lists, "at the gym" status and leaderboards according to your settings.
  • Safety and moderation: review reports, enforce our Community Guidelines, filter abusive language, and suspend or remove accounts that break the rules.
  • Communicate with you: account and security notices, replies to support requests and, only with your opt-in, marketing emails.
  • Improve BOATS: fix bugs and understand which features are used, using information about how the app performs.
  • Legal: comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and protect the rights and safety of our users and BOATS.

6. Emails and marketing

  • Account and service emails (for example security notices or replies to your support request) are sent when needed.
  • Marketing emails (news, tips, offers and promotions, which may include offers from brands we partner with) are sent only if you opt in by turning on Settings → Emails from BOATS. It is off by default.
  • You can opt out anytime by turning that setting off or by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Partners never receive your email address or personal information from us. If an email includes a partner offer, it is sent by BOATS.

7. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With other users, as described in the next section and controlled by your settings.
  • With service providers who process data for us under contract:
    ProviderWhat it does
    Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting)Sign-in, database and hosting for all app data.
    GitHubRuns our scheduled maintenance and moderation jobs.
    ntfy.shDelivers moderation alerts to the BOATS team when a new public post is made. Alerts contain only the poster's username, the first words of the caption and a link.
    Email service provider (when marketing emails launch)Sends emails to people who opted in.
    Apple and GoogleApp distribution and, in the store apps, Sign in with Apple and optional Apple Health or Health Connect features.
  • For legal reasons: to comply with law or a valid legal request, to enforce our Terms, or to protect anyone's safety.
  • In a business transfer: if BOATS is merged, acquired or sells assets, your information may transfer under this policy, and we will tell you before it becomes subject to a different policy.

8. What other users can see

  • Always visible to signed-in users: your username, display name, profile photo, bio, link, Founding Crew or Early Crew number, follower counts and your public posts with their likes and comments.
  • Visible if you allow it: your town, height, weight, goal, "at the gym" status, streak and ranking, and your weekly split (which others can copy).
  • Private to you: your email address, workouts you save privately, body weight history, sex, injury flags, checklist and settings.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you message, and to the BOATS team when needed to investigate a report or enforce our rules.

9. Safety and moderation

To keep BOATS safe, the BOATS team can review public posts, comments, profiles, and the content of reported conversations, and may remove content or suspend or delete accounts that break our Community Guidelines. We are notified of new public posts so we can review them quickly. You can report any post, comment or account, and block anyone, from inside the app.

10. Storage and security

Your data is stored by Google Firebase in the United States and protected by encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, by access rules that limit each user to their own private data, and by restricted administrative access. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

11. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Walk the plank → Delete. This permanently deletes your account, profile, workouts, posts, comments, likes, messages you sent, photos and username, and closes your sign-in. You can download your data first.
  • Without the app: see carrytheboats.app/delete-account or email us from the address on your account.
  • Deleted data is removed from our live systems right away and from backups within 30 days. We may keep limited records when required by law, to resolve disputes, or for safety (for example, records that an account was removed for abuse).

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states, the EEA or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct, download, delete or restrict use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You can do most of this in the app: edit your profile, change sharing settings, download your data, turn marketing emails off, and delete your account. For anything else, email boatsapplication@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on contract (to provide the Service), consent (marketing emails, optional health connections) and legitimate interests (safety, security and improvement).

13. Children

BOATS is not for children under 13, and you must be at least 16 to use BOATS in the EEA or the UK unless a parent consents where the law allows. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BOATS grows. If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

15. Contact

Questions, requests or concerns: boatsapplication@gmail.com.
