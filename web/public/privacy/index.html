This Privacy Policy explains how BOATS ("BOATS", "we", "us" or "our"), the workout tracking and social fitness app available at https://carrytheboats.app, collects, uses, shares, stores and protects your information, including information we receive from Google when you sign in with your Google account. By using BOATS you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

BOATS is an independently developed fitness app currently in beta. It lets you plan a weekly training split, log workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights, cardio and duration), track progress and streaks, and share workouts and photos with friends who also use BOATS. BOATS is the data controller for the information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

  • Account and profile: username, optional real name, optional bio, optional website link, optional town, profile photo, and Founding Crew number if you were one of the first 100 members.
  • Body and training details (optional): height, body weight, weigh-in history, goal weight, sex, training goal (bulk, maintain or cut), experience level, preferred rest time and rep ranges. Sex is used only to estimate calories and is never shown to other users.
  • Workout data: your weekly split, planned exercises, logged sets, reps, weights, equipment, cardio settings, workout start and end times, calories estimates, streaks, personal records, and photos you attach to workouts.
  • Social content: posts and captions, photos, likes, comments and replies, people you follow, pinned posts, mentions, direct messages (text, photos and voice notes), and the read status of messages.
  • Reports and feedback: reports you submit about posts or users, and feedback you send through the in-app Feedback button.
  • Moderation choices: people you block or mute and posts you hide.

b) Information we receive from Google

See Section 3 for exactly what Google sign-in shares with us and how it is used.

c) Information collected automatically

  • Account activity: the date you created your account and the last time you used BOATS.
  • Gym status: whether you have a workout in progress, used to show an "at the gym" indicator if you choose to share it.
  • Technical data: our hosting and sign-in provider (Google Firebase) processes standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and device information to deliver the service, keep sign-in secure and prevent abuse. We do not use this information to track you across other websites or apps.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, health data from other apps, payment information, or advertising identifiers. BOATS does not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and secure your BOATS account as described above. We do not sell it, rent it, use it for advertising, use it to build marketing profiles, or use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to anyone except our service provider Google Firebase, which stores it on our behalf, or when required by law.

  • You can revoke BOATS's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the Google user data we already store, follow Section 8.

4. How we use information

  • Provide the app: create your account, save and sync your workouts across devices, show your progress, streaks and charts, and display your profile and posts.
  • Personalize training: suggest weights, rep ranges and rest times, estimate calories burned using your body weight, height and (if provided) sex, and recommend splits for your experience level.
  • Social features: build your feed, show likes and comments, let you follow people, send and receive messages, show notifications inside the app, and show leaderboards.
  • Safety and moderation: review reports, enforce our Terms of Service, and prevent spam and abuse. When needed for safety, the BOATS team may review the content of accounts, posts, workouts and messages, and may suspend or remove accounts.
  • Communicate with you: respond to your feedback and send in-app messages about your account.
  • Improve BOATS: fix bugs and decide what to build next using feedback you send us.

We process your information because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for, based on your consent (for optional details and Google sign-in), and for our legitimate interest in keeping BOATS safe and working.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • With other BOATS users, as described in Section 6, based on what you choose to make public or send to someone.
  • With our service provider Google Firebase (Google LLC), which provides sign-in, hosting and the database that stores BOATS data on our behalf, under Google's data processing terms.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, legal process or to protect the rights, safety or property of BOATS, our users or the public.
  • If BOATS is transferred as part of a merger, acquisition or sale, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. What other users can see

  • Always visible to signed-in BOATS users: your username, profile photo, display name (if you add one), bio, link, follower and following counts, posts you share, and your likes and comments.
  • Visible only if you turn it on in Settings: town, training goal, height, body weight, gym status and streak, and your weekly split.
  • Private to you: your email address, sex, workout history you don't post, weigh-ins, goal weight, and blocked or muted lists.
  • Direct messages are visible to you and the person you message (and to the BOATS team only for safety reviews).

7. Storage and security

BOATS data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) on Google Cloud servers. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google. Database security rules limit access so that private data can only be read by you, and by the BOATS owner account for moderation. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

8. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information for as long as your account is active.
  • To delete your account and data, send a deletion request using the Feedback button in the app or the contact details in Section 14. We will delete your profile, workouts, posts, photos, likes, comments, messages and the Google user data we store within 30 days, and confirm when it is done.
  • You can delete individual posts, comments and messages at any time inside the app.
  • We may keep limited information longer when required by law or to prevent abuse (for example, a record that an account was removed for breaking our rules).

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correct: view and edit your profile, body details and settings in the app at any time.
  • Control visibility: choose what others can see in Settings, keep workouts private, and block, mute or report other users.
  • Export: download a PDF report of your workouts from Settings, or ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Delete: delete content in the app or request full account deletion (Section 8).
  • Withdraw consent: remove optional details at any time, and revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights such as objecting to processing or lodging a complaint with a data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Cookies and local storage

BOATS uses your browser's local storage and the sign-in session storage provided by Firebase to keep you signed in, remember settings like sound and theme, and work offline. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Children

BOATS is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International users

BOATS is operated from the United States and data is processed on Google Cloud servers that may be located in the United States or other countries. By using BOATS you understand your information may be processed outside your country, with the protections described in this policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BOATS grows. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for important changes, notify you in the app before they take effect.

14. Contact us

For privacy questions, data requests or account deletion, contact BOATS:

  • In the app: tap the Feedback button on any screen.
  • Website: https://carrytheboats.app
